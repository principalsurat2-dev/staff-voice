Staff Voice

A confidential channel for wellbeing, workload, and the good practice happening in our classrooms. No names. No logins. No sign-in trail.

This form collects no name, email, or device identifier. Responses are pooled and only ever reviewed in aggregate, never per submission timestamp order shared with wing heads.

Why this exists: the best improvements to our school culture come from what teachers actually experience day to day — not what leadership assumes. This form takes about 5 minutes.

What we do with it: responses are reviewed by the Principal's office once every reporting cycle, summarised as themes and numbers, and shared back with staff — what we heard, and what we're changing because of it.

What we don't do: we don't try to identify who wrote what. If a response is detailed enough to identify a specific person, department, or incident, treat it as a starting point for a conversation you'd want to have anyway — not a report card on any individual.
